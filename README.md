## What it does

Consumer credit needed a bureau. Autonomous AI needs one too.

TAFAAR turns `.sova` evidence into:

- **The TRUSCOR Rating:** a private score from **300 to 900**.
- **An Estimated Financial Exposure:** what the AI system's risk could cost.
- **A risk map** across rules and regulations.

## Why it's never licensed

TRUSCOR stays neutral: no product in any category it rates, no referral economics, no certification today and no insurance on its balance sheet. Keeping the grading engine in-house means one standard, applied the same way to everyone.

## Where it fits in the SOVA stack

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| [SOVA Engine](https://github.com/TRUSCOR/SOVA-Engine) | Tests AI products and produces `.sova` evidence |
| [SOVA Black Box](https://github.com/TRUSCOR/SOVA-Blackbox) | Records AI systems in production |
| [O.W.L](https://github.com/TRUSCOR/O.W.L) | The measurement instrument: checks claims against real effects |
| **TAFAAR** | Grades the evidence into the TRUSCOR Rating |
| [SOVA OSS](https://github.com/TRUSCOR/SOVA-OSS) | The free, open-source toolkit and the open `.sova` format |
